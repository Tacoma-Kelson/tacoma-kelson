# Tacoma Kelson

## About Me

Hi, I'm Tacoma Kelson. I am a Finance major at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa with a concentration in Real Estate. I am interested in real estate, mortgage lending, and helping people better understand their financial options when buying a home.

I also have experience in social media and content creation, where I have worked with local Hawaiʻi brands to create promotional content. I am interested in how I can combine my background in finance, real estate, and social media as I begin my career.

## Career Goals

After graduating, I hope to work in the mortgage and real estate industry in Hawaiʻi. One of my main goals is to help local families better understand the homebuying process and the different resources available to them.
