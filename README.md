# Tacoma Kelson

## About Me

Hi, I'm Tacoma Kelson. I am a Finance major at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa with a concentration in Real Estate. I am interested in real estate, mortgage lending, and helping people better understand their financial options when buying a home. Through my classes, I have been able to learn more about finance and real estate and how they connect to decisions people make when buying or investing in property.

I also have experience in social media and content creation, where I have worked with local Hawaiʻi brands to create promotional content. This experience has helped me build my communication, marketing, and creative skills. I am interested in how I can combine my background in finance, real estate, and social media as I begin my career. I also think social media can be a useful way to make information about real estate and homeownership easier for people to understand.

## Career Goals

After graduating, I hope to work in the mortgage and real estate industry in Hawaiʻi. I chose this career because homeownership can be difficult for many local families, especially with the high cost of living in Hawaiʻi. I want to help people understand the homebuying process, learn about the resources available to them, and make financial decisions that are best for their situation. Being able to help local families work toward owning a home in Hawaiʻi is something that would make this career meaningful to me.

## Portfolio

This portfolio will include projects and work that show my experience in finance, real estate, data analysis, and other skills I develop throughout my coursework. It will also show my growth as I continue learning and gaining experience that will prepare me for my future career.
