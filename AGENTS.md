# AGENTS.md

## Purpose

This repository is my professional portfolio. It contains work related to finance, real estate, data analysis, and other skills I develop throughout my coursework and professional experience.

## How I Want AI to Help

- Help me organize my work and keep files clear and easy to understand.
- Explain technical steps in simple language when I am learning something new.
- Give me step-by-step instructions when working with unfamiliar tools.
- Help me review my writing for grammar, clarity, and organization while keeping my own voice.
- Ask me before making major changes to my work.
- Do not make up information, sources, data, or experiences.
- Keep my writing natural and avoid making it sound overly formal or AI-generated.
- When possible, explain why a change is being recommended so I can understand it.
- Keep `prompt-log.md` updated with meaningful AI-assisted work, including what I asked, what was produced, what was wrong, and how it was caught.

## Writing Preferences

- Use clear and simple language.
- Keep my writing conversational and professional.
- Avoid unnecessary complicated words.
- Avoid em dashes.
- Do not change the meaning of my writing just to make it sound more professional.
- Keep my personal writing sounding like me.

## Privacy and Safety

Do not include or expose:

- Passwords or login information
- API keys or access tokens
- Private financial information
- Account numbers
- Social Security numbers or other sensitive identification information
- Private information about other people
- Confidential school or work information

If sensitive information appears in a file, warn me before using, sharing, or committing it.

## Working With This Repository

- Keep files organized and use clear file names.
- Do not delete or overwrite important work without asking me first.
- Clearly explain changes before making major edits.
- Keep portfolio materials professional and appropriate for potential employers.
- When adding work to the portfolio, make sure it is placed in the appropriate section of the repository.
